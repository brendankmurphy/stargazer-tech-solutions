Five sports, one focus

We work where the race is against the clock, the course, and yourself. Staying in one corner of sport lets us learn it properly.

  • Swimming

    Age-group and high school meets, masters, and open water.

  • Track and field

    Sprints, distance, hurdles, jumps, and throws.

  • Cross country

    Invitationals through championship season, on any course.

  • Ultra-marathoning

    50K to 100 miles and beyond, on road and trail.

  • Triathlon

    Sprint to full distance, across swim, bike, and run.

Native on iOS and Android

We build for both platforms, so athletes, coaches, and families can use the same app whichever phone they carry.

iOS

Apps for iPhone and iPad, designed to Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and distributed through the App Store.

Android

Apps for Android phones and tablets, designed to Material Design guidelines and distributed through Google Play.

Both, in step

One product plan and one release schedule, so neither platform waits on the other.

How a project runs

Four stages, from the first conversation to an app in the stores.

  1. Talk

    Tell us about your sport, your athletes, and the problem you want solved.

  2. Plan

    We agree on scope, platforms, timeline, and cost before any code is written.

  3. Build

    You try working builds on your own phone as the app takes shape.

  4. Launch

    We handle App Store and Google Play submission, then stay on for support and updates.
